## Cold prefill and decode at depth

One completion request per depth: a calibrated random word-list prompt, then 256 forced decode tokens; prefill rate is `usage.prompt_time` with `cached_tokens == 0`.

| prompt tokens | prefill (tok/s) | decode after it (tok/s) |
|---|---|---|
| 8,200 | 1,383 | 23.9 |
| 32,772 | 1,676 | 21.3 |
| 130,998 | 1,758 | 41.0 |

The low decode rates after the 8k and 32k prompts were explained in R870: the first requests after a boot route the word-list filler to experts the placement does not hold on the GPU, and the rate recovers as placement adapts (`r870-glm53-depth.md`).
