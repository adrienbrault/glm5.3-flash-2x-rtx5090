Results directory on the box: `/srv/qwen5090/results/2026-10-08-r923-glm53-l2-150647/`. Driver: `scripts/r923-glm53-l2-green.sh` (helpers `scripts/glm_arms.sh`). Engine logs are not published. The overlay (`sm120-l2-green-r1`, L2 half only, written by an OpenAI Codex agent, native extension rebuilt) is not in this repository.

## What the overlay does

`EXL3_SM120_L2=1` sets CUDA access-policy windows that mark a per-step working set (state the decode kernels re-read) as persisting in the GPU's L2, on the streams and the CUDA-graph nodes of the decode path. Off by default.

## Probes before the served arms

- Both RTX 5090 accept persisting L2: 96 MiB of L2, at most 60 MiB persisting, windows up to 128 MiB, on streams and on graph nodes. A re-read microbenchmark went from 0.0275 to 0.0252 ms on GPU0 and from 0.0281 to 0.0266 ms on GPU1, output byte-equal.
- The native gate (`G/`): one layer's kernels at 1 and 4 rows on each GPU, 106 tensors per case bitwise equal with and without the window, 0.000004 to 0.000062 ms per layer apart.
- The L2 hit-rate counter was not available to the profiler on this box (`H/hit-rate-status.json`), so no hit rate was measured.

## Reading

- Against the mean of the two arms without it, the window lowered the c1 score by 17.6 %, the distinct c1 round by 13.2 %, the 2-stream sum by 27.5 % and the 4-stream sum by 10.4 %; the server step rose by 3.3 ms per token (19.63 against 16.30 ms per token). The spread between OFF1 and OFF2 was 4.4 % on the c1 score and 1.2 % at c4.
- The round did not measure why. Two candidates: lines held as persisting leave less L2 for the rest of the step's data, which the decode kernels also re-read, and setting the window costs host time per launch.
- Not served, and no smaller setting was tried: the loss is larger than any gain the microbenchmark showed.
- Both arms without the window measured 83.6 and 84.6 tok/s at c4, below the served configuration's 90.0 to 93.5 in R915c, R925 and R927, all in other sessions; this round's comparison is within its own arms only.
